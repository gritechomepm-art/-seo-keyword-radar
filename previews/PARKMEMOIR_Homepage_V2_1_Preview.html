02 · SUV CARGO FIT

Will a 12V fridge fit my SUV?

Start with a vehicle reference, then check actual cargo dimensions and tailgate clearance before choosing a fridge.

↔

Toyota RAV4 selected as a reference only. The complete fit calculator is in review; use the measurement checklist before choosing a fridge.

Measure these five clearances in your vehicle
  1. Usable cargo floor depth to the closed hatch
  2. Narrowest cargo floor width
  3. Usable vertical height at the fridge position
  4. Tailgate opening width at its narrowest point
  5. Tailgate opening height, plus room to open the fridge lid

Presets are planning references, not fit guarantees. Verify trim, seats, lid clearance and ventilation in your actual SUV.

FAMILY TRIP FIELD NOTES

How to plan a national park road trip with kids

Begin with the experience your family wants, not a long list of stops. Choose a park and travel month, then check official winter access and seasonal services before booking a room or campsite. Road conditions, shuttle schedules and visitor-center hours can change within the same month, so confirm details again close to departure.

Next, plan a flexible daily rhythm: one meaningful outing, a food break and an indoor or short-walk alternative if weather or energy levels change. Our Zion with kids guide and three-day Zion itinerary show how to balance stops with family downtime.

Pack for the actual vehicle and overnight setup. Measure the cargo area before buying a large 12V fridge, and estimate battery needs only if your trip will be away from reliable outlets. Finally, give children their own regional park journal to record what they noticed, not just where they went.
